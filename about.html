The insight behind Anchor

GLP-1 and peptide therapy works by quieting hunger signals. For people managing weight, metabolic health, or body composition, that's exactly the point. The problem is that reduced appetite doesn't come with a reduced requirement for the nutrients your body still needs in full.

Most people on semaglutide, tirzepatide, or other peptide protocols eat 40–60% less than they did before starting. Their electrolyte intake drops. Their protein intake drops. Vitamins, minerals, fiber — all reduced alongside the meals. The body doesn't adjust its requirements; only the intake changes.

Anchor was built around that one insight. Not as a general wellness supplement, and not as a meal replacement. As a targeted protocol for the specific gaps that peptide therapy creates.

Three formulas. One for GI support. One for muscle preservation. One for micronutrient coverage. Each ingredient clinically dosed. Nothing proprietary, nothing hidden.

How we build formulas

Every ingredient in every Anchor formula has a specific job and a published dose. We don't use proprietary blends — the practice of listing impressive ingredients on a label without disclosing amounts. We list everything.

Dosing is based on peer-reviewed clinical research, not what fits the cheapest unit economics. When a clinical trial found that 500mg of ginger root extract (standardized to 5% gingerols) reduced nausea in a controlled population, that's the dose we use in GI Relief. Every ingredient has the same standard.

Every production lot is independently third-party tested. What's on the label is in the product.

Anchor is for anyone whose appetite has been meaningfully reduced by peptide therapy — semaglutide, tirzepatide, or similar — and who wants to make sure their nutrition keeps pace with their goals.
