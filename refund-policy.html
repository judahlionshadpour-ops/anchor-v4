Refund Policy

ANCHOR stands behind its formulas with a first-order guarantee. Because our products are ingested, our process differs from a typical apparel or hard-goods return — read the clauses below before requesting a refund.

1. Who this covers

This guarantee applies to your first individual-formula order or first Full Bundle order only. Reorders, subsequent subscription renewals, and repeat purchases are final sale — see Clause 6.

2. Window to request

Reach out between day 15 and day 30 after your delivery date. We ask for at least two weeks of use first — a single dose isn't a fair test of a daily-use formula, and requests outside this window won't be honored.

3. What qualifies

A refund applies when the product genuinely didn't work as described for you, or caused an unwanted reaction. It does not cover:

  • Taste, texture, or mixability preference
  • Change of mind or ordering the wrong formula
  • Not following the label's directions for use
  • Any subscription renewal or repeat order (Clause 6)
  • Discounted, final-sale, or discontinued batches
  • Bulk, wholesale, or resale purchases

4. Multi-box orders

If your first order contained more than one box (e.g. a multi-pack), the guarantee covers a single box's worth of the price paid. Additional boxes in that same order are final sale.

5. One per household

We honor one refund per customer or household within any 12-month period.

6. Subscription renewals are final

Once a renewal box has been charged and shipped, that charge is final — we cannot refund a box already in transit. To stop a future renewal from charging at all, cancel or pause before your next billing date; see our Cancellation Policy.

7. Don't ship anything back

Because this is a consumable product, we don't accept physical returns — opened or unopened. Do not mail product back to us; it will not be processed. Approved refunds are issued without requiring a return shipment.

8. Damaged, wrong, or missing items

Different rule, different clock: report a damaged box, wrong formula, or missing item within 7 days of delivery, with a photo of the packaging and product. We'll send a free replacement or refund, your choice — no guarantee window applies to our mistakes.

9. If you have a reaction

Stop using the product and consult a healthcare provider if needed. Separately, let us know so we can document what happened — we review every report individually.

10. How to actually request one

Email judahlionshadpour@gmail.com with your order number, the email used at checkout, and your reason. We handle refunds by email only — we can't process requests made by phone, text, or social media DM.

11. Chargebacks

Please contact us before filing a chargeback with your bank — most issues are resolved within a few business days once we hear from you. Filing a dispute without reaching out first skips a process that would likely have resolved things faster. If a chargeback is filed regardless, we'll respond with your order records and this policy as evidence; a denied dispute doesn't waive your right to still submit a normal refund request under the terms above.

12. How refunds are paid out

  • Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method only
  • Typical processing: 5-10 business days from approval, plus however long your bank takes to post it
  • Prepaid or virtual debit cards may not be able to receive a refund at all — check with your card issuer
  • If your original payment method or account has since closed, we may not be able to complete the refund
  • Original shipping charges (where applicable) are not refunded

Last updated: August 2026. This policy does not affect any statutory rights you may have under applicable law.
