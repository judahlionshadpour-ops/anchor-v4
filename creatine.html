Creatine Monohydrate

Pure, research-backed muscle preservation for weight loss, aging, and performance.

What is creatine monohydrate?

Creatine is a compound naturally found in muscle tissue that regenerates ATP — the cell's primary energy currency. Supplementing with creatine monohydrate increases muscle creatine stores, enabling muscles to work harder, recover faster, and resist breakdown during periods of reduced calorie intake or aging.

It is one of the most extensively researched dietary supplements in existence: over 700 peer-reviewed studies consistently demonstrate its safety and efficacy across diverse populations, from elite athletes to older adults managing sarcopenia.

Why creatine?

On GLP-1 therapy, your body loses 20–40% of weight reduction as unintended lean muscle mass. Creatine monohydrate specifically addresses the energy side of muscle preservation: it sustains ATP regeneration during the reduced training intensity and food intake that accompanies peptide therapy.

  • 5–10% strength gains over 6–8 weeks, independent of athletic status
  • 1–3 lbs additional lean mass preservation compared to training alone
  • Faster recovery — reduced delayed-onset muscle soreness (DOMS)
  • Efficacy independent of timing — consistency over weeks matters, not daily timing
  • Extremely cost-effective — $0.03–$0.15 per gram vs. $0.40–$1.20 for whey protein

How to use

Dosing: 5 g per day, any time. No loading phase required. Full muscle saturation occurs in 3–4 weeks of consistent daily dosing.

Mixing: Mix with 8–12 oz water or your preferred beverage. Creatine is slightly gritty; stir or shake well. The taste is neutral.

Hydration: Drink adequate water throughout the day. Creatine draws water into muscle cells (beneficial for force production), so maintain baseline hydration, especially if on GLP-1 therapy.

Timing: Creatine absorption is not timing-dependent. Take it with breakfast, before bed, or split doses — whatever you'll remember consistently.

Safety

Creatine monohydrate is among the most extensively tested and well-tolerated supplements. A meta-analysis of 684 randomized controlled trials involving over 12,800 participants found "very rare" serious adverse events and confirmed no long-term toxicity at recommended doses.

  • No kidney damage in healthy individuals — multiple studies confirm creatinine levels remain normal with consistent use
  • No cancer risk, no muscle cramping — these claims are unsupported by controlled research
  • Intramuscular water retention only — you gain 1–2 kg water weight in muscle tissue (beneficial), not subcutaneous fluid
  • ISSN 2025 consensus: Safe for healthy adults at 3–5 g/day

Note: If you have pre-existing kidney disease, consult your physician and obtain a baseline creatinine level before starting.
