Usage & Safety
Anchor contains no ingredients with known interactions with GLP-1 receptor agonists (semaglutide, tirzepatide, liraglutide, etc.). We always recommend confirming any new supplement with your prescribing physician, especially if you are on other medications.
GI Relief is most effective taken with a meal or when nausea is present. Muscle Guard is best within an hour of physical activity or with your largest meal. Complete can be taken any time — most customers take it in the morning with their first meal or coffee.

Anchor was formulated specifically for the gaps created by reduced appetite on GLP-1 therapy. The formulas are also effective for anyone eating significantly below their typical intake — intermittent fasting, post-bariatric recovery, or very low calorie periods — but the intended user is someone on a peptide protocol.
