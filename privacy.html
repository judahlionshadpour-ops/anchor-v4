Privacy Policy

How Anchor Nutrition collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: August 12, 2026

1. Who we are

Anchor Nutrition, Inc. ("Anchor," "we," "us," or "our") operates youranchor.co. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website and how we use it.

2. Information we collect

We collect information in two ways:

  • Information you provide — name, email address, shipping address, and payment details when you place an order or contact us.
  • Information collected automatically — browser type, pages visited, time on site, referring URL, and device type via analytics tools described below.

3. Analytics and session recording

We use the following third-party tools to understand how visitors use our site. These tools may collect and process data about your browsing behavior.

Google Analytics 4 (GA4)
We use Google Analytics to measure traffic sources, page views, and user behavior in aggregate. Google Analytics may use cookies and similar technologies. Data is processed by Google LLC. You can opt out via Google's opt-out browser add-on. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Microsoft Clarity
We use Microsoft Clarity to record anonymized session replays and generate heatmaps showing how visitors interact with our pages — including mouse movements, clicks, and scroll depth. Clarity may record your interactions with this website. The data collected does not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive personal information. Microsoft Clarity's data is processed by Microsoft Corporation. You can learn more about Clarity's data practices at clarity.microsoft.com/terms and Microsoft's privacy policy at privacy.microsoft.com.

4. Cookies

Our analytics tools use cookies — small text files stored in your browser — to recognize returning visitors and track session data. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some site features may not function as expected.

5. How we use your information

  • To fulfill orders and provide customer support
  • To improve site content, layout, and user experience
  • To understand which pages and products interest our visitors
  • To send order confirmations and transactional emails

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

6. Data retention

Order and customer data is retained for up to 7 years for legal and accounting purposes. Analytics data is retained per each tool's default retention settings (26 months for GA4; session recordings in Clarity are retained for 90 days by default).

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@youranchor.co. Residents of California (CCPA) and the European Economic Area (GDPR) may have additional rights.

8. Children's privacy

Our site is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
