Subscription Cancellation Policy

The Full Anchor subscription renews automatically each month until you stop it. Here's exactly how stopping it works.

1. Automatic renewal

Your subscription bills and ships on the same date each month unless you cancel before that date arrives. There's no fixed contract length and no minimum number of shipments.

2. Cancelling yourself, anytime

Open your subscription management portal (linked in your confirmation and receipt emails) and cancel with one click — no phone call, no retention screen, no reason required.

3. The cutoff that matters

Cancel any time before your next billing date and you won't be charged again. Cancel on or after that date and the cycle already in motion still processes — cancellation takes effect for the following cycle, not the one already underway.

4. What cancelling doesn't do

Stopping future billing doesn't unwind a charge that's already gone through. A renewal box that's been charged and shipped is final sale — see our Refund Policy for what's covered if that box arrives damaged, wrong, or missing.

5. Want a break instead of stopping entirely?

Skip-a-month and pause aren't self-serve in the portal yet — email us before your next billing date and we'll hold that cycle for you manually. (If you'd rather just cancel and resubscribe later, that's always available instantly through the portal.)

6. Changing card, address, or plan

Update your payment method or shipping address anytime from the same portal. To change formulas or switch between the monthly plan and a one-time order, cancel the current subscription and place a new order — plan-switching isn't automated yet.

7. Support

Can't access your portal, or need the manual pause above? Email judahlionshadpour@gmail.com with your order number and the email used at checkout.
